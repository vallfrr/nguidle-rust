name: ngu-verify
description: Verify an engine change and record reproducible evidence.
---

Run `just check` and keep a short summary of the result. Exercise the
affected path with the development guide in `docs/DEV.md`. Sample the
changed screens and relevant display modes for visual fixes. Use isolated
saves and record what was compared, what ran on hardware and what remains
unverified. Follow the issue and pull request workflow in `AGENTS.md`.
